## Included Products

| Product | Visual Direction |
| --- | --- |
| LT Mini | Compact MVP handheld with LCD, joystick, LoRa, USB-C, and expansion direction. |
| LT Core | Larger flagship handheld with keyboard, joystick, larger display, and internal expansion direction. |
| LT Core Pro | Future higher-end Core variant with compute-module, Linux, dock, and shared-bus direction. |

## Rules

- Treat this board as a communication tool, not manufacturing data.
- Validate real dimensions before PCB or enclosure work.
- Keep LT Mini visually simple and MVP-focused.
- Keep LT Core Pro as an extension of LT Core, not a separate ecosystem.
