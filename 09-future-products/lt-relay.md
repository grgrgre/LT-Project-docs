# LT Relay

LT Relay is a future expansion product concept for radio, network, or automation
experiments.

## Possible Roles

- LoRa relay node
- test device for mesh-network research
- fixed-location radio bridge
- automation or sensor gateway

## Design Rules

- Keep protocol compatibility with LT handheld devices.
- Document power source assumptions.
- Treat mesh behavior as research until packet framing is proven.
- Avoid making LT Relay required for basic LT Mini operation.
