# LT Dock

LT Dock is a future accessory concept for desktop, bench, power, and I/O
workflows.

## Possible Roles

- stable desktop power
- USB or serial access
- firmware development
- diagnostics
- module testing
- external I/O expansion

## Design Rules

- Do not require the dock for normal handheld operation.
- Keep electrical limits documented.
- Avoid proprietary assumptions before the base connector is defined.
- Support development and testing workflows first.
