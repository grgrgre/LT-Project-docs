# LT Extreme

LT Extreme is a future experimental concept for a rugged or high-capability LT
variant.

This product should stay out of MVP planning until LT Mini and LT Core
requirements are clearer.

## Possible Direction

- rugged enclosure
- larger battery
- stronger radio or antenna options
- environmental sealing research
- field-focused accessories

## Constraint

LT Extreme must not drive early architecture decisions unless the same decision
also benefits LT Mini or LT Core.
